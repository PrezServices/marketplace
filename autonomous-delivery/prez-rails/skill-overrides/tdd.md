# tdd: our changes

- **What to mock and how tests are structured** come from the repo's own
  testing conventions: its `CLAUDE.md`, and the Patterns and Gates of its page
  in `.prez-rails/repos/<repo>.md`. They outrank `tdd/mocking.md` and
  `tdd/tests.md`; read them before the first test. Where the repo has no
  convention, follow the skill and name the convention you chose in the PR.
- **Seams named in the OpenSpec change** (`design.md` or `tasks.md`) or in the
  ticket's Tech decisions count as pre-agreed. Ask a person to confirm seams
  only when neither names them.
